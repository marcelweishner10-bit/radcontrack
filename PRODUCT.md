# Product

## Register
product

## Users and purpose
Radiology duty staff at BTHDC record daily contrast, film usage and stock movements. The department head reconciles receipts, use and remaining stock each month. Staff need a simple form during their shift, while reports provide calculations and trends.

## Design principles
Use plain operational labels. Keep entry forms focused on numbers staff actually record. Show film sizes separately for each modality. Calculate totals automatically. Keep analytical averages in reports. Require confirmed stock counts rather than guessing physical balances.

## Confirmed scope

The approved November workflow keeps contrast (patients, administered ml and wastage) per shift. Night personnel close films by room and size, plus all-room CDs, sleeves, connectors and injector syringes once daily from the procedure sheets. Existing shift records retain their format and quantities. Daily totals include earlier shift usage and deduct only the difference; never rewrite or deduct earlier entries twice. Packs/bottles finished are optional daily entries, electrodes are checked weekly with an open-pack note, and markers/pens/A4 remain issue-tracked outside the daily checklist. Pickups and top-ups still retain date, shift, recipient and destination, accumulate automatically and remain separate from consumption.
The user approved simplifying film entry to patients printed for, 17 × 14 films and 12 × 10 films. Maintain the existing appearance and input accessibility; remove the per-patient averages from the entry table.

The user also approved a dedicated Daily usage home page, separate received-stock management for authorised editors, plain count labels, corrections beside movement records, separate balances/reports/history pages, and an authorised audit-history screen. Preserve both available themes and use the existing components rather than changing the visual identity.

The latest approved workflow connects new contrast and film entries to room stock once. Contrast retains patient counts and actual administered ml, with a separate wastage field. Films have patient counts per size. Show carried-over stock, pickups and remaining stock, with multiple top-ups per shift. Save progress is separate from reviewed shift completion. Keep earlier clinical records without deducting them again, and never guess physical balances or missing historical patient breakdowns. Printer paper is administrative department stock, not routine room usage. Gloves use packs and must remain visible in the stock summary.
