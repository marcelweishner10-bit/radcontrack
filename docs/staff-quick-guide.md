# Tracstoc: quick guide for duty radiographers

## Every shift (all rooms: X-ray, CT, MRI, Fluoroscopy, Mammography)
1. Open **Daily usage**, then choose the date, shift and your room.
2. Enter your name in **Recorded by**.
3. For each item, enter what **this room** used, or press **None used**.
   - Contrast: ml given to patients, wastage in ml, and the number of patients.
   - Films: films printed for each size, including reprints, and the patients printed for at each size.
   - Other consumables: pieces or packs used.
4. **Save progress** any time; it does not finish the shift.
5. At handover, press **Finish shift**. You do **not** need to count stock to finish.

## When you collect stock from the department store
- Go to **Stock → Pick for a room** and choose who it is for, the shift, the items and the quantities.
- You can put everything in one pick. MRI contrast goes to MRI, CT contrast to CT, and everything else to **Shared stock** automatically.
- A pick moves stock. It is **not** usage; record usage on the daily page.

## Shared stock
- Films, CDs, sleeves, gloves, connectors and Gastrolux are one pool for all rooms.
- In your table, **Used in other rooms this shift** shows what others took from the same pool. Enter only your own room's use.

## If the app says usage is more than the recorded stock
- A pickup was probably not recorded. Record it under **Pick for a room** first, then save your usage.
- Pickups for an earlier day go to Honey.

## Corrections
- You can correct today and yesterday. Ask **Honey** for older days, past pickups, or contrast recorded in the wrong room.
- Every correction keeps the original in the audit history.

## Stock check (Honey, or as agreed)
- Go to **Stock → Stock check**, choose **Shared stock** or a contrast room, and enter only what you physically counted.
- The table shows the calculated amount, what you counted and the difference.
- A count **replaces** the balance at that location. It does not add stock.
