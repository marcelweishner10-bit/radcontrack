# TrackRad visual identity

Radiology duty staff work in bright daytime rooms and dim night shifts. Retain the existing light/dark choice, clinical tables and operational layout.

Use a restrained teal and mint palette. Teal marks actions and links; mint marks selected controls in dark mode. Pale green-tinted surfaces and deep green text serve light mode. Dark teal surfaces and near-white green-tinted text serve dark mode. Muted labels must remain readable. Errors stay red and warnings amber; never recolour them as branding.

The approved transparent TrackRad logo is displayed on a pale neutral plate in both themes to preserve the original dark lettering. Keep its aspect ratio and accessible name. Use the existing system typography and component spacing. Shared colour tokens use OKLCH and apply consistently to navigation, forms, tables and report tooltips.
